# Faire for group orders (22 Oct 2026)

## What Faire is, for this feature

Faire is a wholesale marketplace: independent retailers buy from 100,000+ brands at wholesale prices. Each brand sets its own **first-order minimum** and a usually lower **reorder minimum**; minimums are optional for brands, and available-to-ship and preorder items can be combined at checkout to reach one. Retailers get 60-day payment terms when eligible and free returns on a first order from any brand. Faire has **no group-buying feature**: it does not pool several shops' baskets to reach a minimum. That is the gap a group chat can fill: one retailer account (the owner) places the order; the people in the chat each say what they want; the assistant keeps the running total against the brand's minimum and splits the cost.

## The API (verified by probing on 22 Oct 2026)

- Authorise: `https://www.faire.com/oauth2/authorize?applicationId=…&scope=A,B&state=…&redirectUrl=…` (redirects to Faire login; the signed-in Faire user grants scopes).
- Token: `POST https://www.faire.com/api/external-api-oauth2/token` with a snake_case body: `application_token`, `application_secret`, `redirect_url`, `scope` (array), `grant_type: "AUTHORIZATION_CODE"`, `authorization_code`. The endpoint accepted that shape (401 "Invalid credentials" on a bogus code; 400 "parameters cannot be null" on other shapes).
- Calls: `https://www.faire.com/external-api/v2/...` with `X-FAIRE-APP-CREDENTIALS` (base64 `id:secret`) and `X-FAIRE-OAUTH-ACCESS-TOKEN`.
- Endpoints (read on developers.faire.com/docs, 22 Oct 2026, logged in): the External API v2 is **brand-side only**. `GET /brands/profile` (the authorised brand's own profile), `GET /orders`, `GET /orders/{id}`, `PUT .../cancel`, `POST .../items/availability`, `GET .../packing-slip-pdf`, `PUT .../processing`, `POST .../shipments` (orders the brand *received*), inventory / prices / products / prepacks / variants of the brand's own catalogue, and `GET /retailers/public/{retailer_id}`. There is **no brand search, no catalogue browse across brands, and no endpoint for a retailer to create an order.** Scopes are READ_/WRITE_ PRODUCTS, ORDERS, INVENTORIES, plus READ_BRAND, READ_RETAILER, READ_SHIPMENTS, READ_REVIEWS. The OAuth authorise page is meant for a *brand* account; the docs say a single brand should just mint a token in the Brand Portal. Consequence: `FaireRetailer` cannot be implemented against this API; group ordering on Faire needs the operator's retailer account in a browser or a different marketplace. The developer portal has no publish control: "Unpublished" is the listing status of a Custom App ("custom built for a single brand or client"); listing in Faire's integrations directory goes through integrations.support@faire.com and is not needed for OAuth.
- Circle: `src/tools/providers/faire.ts`, `GET /api/connections/faire/link`, `GET /oauth/faire/callback`, Settings → Connections → Connect Faire. App id and secret in `.env` only.

## Best brands: what is knowable without order data

Faire does not publish sales rankings. Signals that exist:
- **Top Shop** (quarterly, 5,000+ brands in the Q1 2026 cohort): earned on shop experience and merchandising, retailer ratings, consistent reorders and on-time fulfilment; Faire says Top Shops average 1.8× the views and 2.4× the revenue of category peers. Q1 2026 names by category: apparel KIM+ONO, CECILIA SÖRENSEN, Stuntin Goods; jewellery General Knot & Co., 4ocean, Océanne; food French Squirrel, Zingerman's Candy Manufactory, Muranda Cheese, XO Marshmallow; home Studio ÜM, Piccolo Seeds, Atlantic Blankets; pets Hugo & Hudson, EzyDog, Smart Cookie Barkery; paper Luca-S, The Unique Paper Company, Sprinkles Studios; beauty Hyssop Beauty Apothecary, Dublin Herbalists; kids Saga Copenhagen, Milk Snob.
- **Bestseller tags and review counts** on faire.com discover pages (e.g. Candy Club's holiday bundle with ~2,300 reviews; Touchland in summer 2026 listings).
- The retailer-side API, once connected, is the real source: the catalogue with minimums, ratings and bestseller flags per brand, which the assistant can rank for the chat's category.

## Design: a group order in a chat

1. **Start.** Someone says "let's do a Faire order from Candy Club". The assistant (owner's Faire connection, owner's shop) reads the brand: minimum, reorder minimum, ships-to, lead time, bestsellers.
2. **Collect.** A shared document (the existing tickable list) with one line per item; each member adds quantities in the chat or on the page. The assistant keeps a running total against the minimum and says what is still missing.
3. **Close.** When the minimum is met and the deadline passes, the assistant shows the basket per person with each share (items, pro-rata shipping), then asks the owner through `request_purchase` (threshold applies). `WRITE_ORDERS` places the order on the owner's account; the confirmation id is the evidence.
4. **Settle and track.** A commitment per person ("pay Alex $42 for the Candy Club order"), nudged by the ledger; shipments watched with `watch_for`; the room told once on delivery.
5. **Recommend.** Before step 1, "what should we order?" ranks brands for the chat's category by Top Shop status, rating, reorder signal and minimum, and names three.

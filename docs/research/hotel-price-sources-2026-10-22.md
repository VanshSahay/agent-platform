# Public hotel price sources for hotel_compare (22 Oct 2026)

The question: Circle shows a hotel's wholesale net (LiteAPI) next to the cheapest public all-in price for the same hotel and dates. Today the public side is Google Hotels through Apify's `kestrel/google-hotels-prices` at $0.004 a hotel row plus $0.002 an offer row, about $0.17–0.28 a comparison at four offers a hotel (six as of today's uncommitted change, so up to $0.40). The owner's ask, in order: a source that is reliable (a named seller's true checkout total for the exact hotel and dates, boutique coverage, fresh), then cheap in dollars and tokens.

Non-negotiables, from today's measurements: the public figure must be an all-in total from a named seller (Booking.com, Expedia, Hotels.com, the hotel's own site); it must cover the hotels LiteAPI returned, matched by name; no per-site browser scraping; low tokens and API cost. Measured on Courchevel: Booking.com's figure on Google's ladder matched Booking's own checkout within $3; Expedia and Hotels.com list before tax on the ladder and Google's hotel-level tax estimate lands within $11 of Expedia's checkout; discount resellers (Super.com, Vio, goseek) list 15–18% under their checkout.

Verified means read from the primary source or observed in a live call today. Inferred is marked.

## 1. LiteAPI's own response

Verified, live call today (`POST /v3.0/hotels/rates`, Courchevel FR, 13–17 Jan 2027, 2 adults, USD, US nationality, limit 5): 1.3 MB, three hotels came back (Ecrin Blanc Resort 4★, Alpes Hôtel du Pralong 5★, Les 3 Vallées a Beaumier hotel 4★), 412 rates. Price fields per rate under `roomTypes[].rates[].retailRate`: `total`, `suggestedSellingPrice` (with `source`), `initialPrice`, `taxesAndFees[] {description, included, amount}`; per offer `offerRetailRate`, `offerInitialPrice`, `suggestedSellingPrice`. Rate level also carries `commission[]`, `providerCommission`, `priceType: "commission"`.

What the docs say ([rates reference](https://docs.liteapi.travel/reference/post_hotels-rates), [Revenue management and commission](https://docs.liteapi.travel/docs/revenue-management-and-commission)):

- `retailRate.total`: "The total cost amount due to book this room. This is what the end user will pay." That is our side of the comparison (net plus the account margin; `margin: 0` on the request "returns a net rate").
- `suggestedSellingPrice`: "The minimum public selling price for all the rooms combined. This is used for pricing control." The revenue page calls it "the rate provided by the hotel as the recommended minimum selling price" and warns that "selling below the SSP in public domains can lead to rate violations." Its worked example is base $100, SSP $115, margin 15.
- `initialPrice`: "the hotel's standard listed price for the room. It is always equal to or higher than the total price."

Observed today: `suggestedSellingPrice / total` was exactly 1.15 on all 412 rates, `source` was `""` on every one, and `initialPrice` equalled `total` everywhere (no strike-through). `taxesAndFees` carried VAT `included: true` and a City Tax mostly `included: false` ($25.70 on the cheapest Ecrin Blanc rate).

Honest reading: SSP is Nuitée's floor for parity, derived by formula here, not a quote from any seller; it is not a "public price" and must not be shown as one. `taxesAndFees` is useful on our side (it names the city tax paid at the hotel). Nothing in `/hotels/rates`, with or without flags, returns a seller's public figure.

Separate endpoint, verified from docs only ([Get cached public price for a hotel](https://docs.liteapi.travel/reference/getpublicprice)): `GET /v3.0/prices/public?hotelId&checkin&checkout&adults[&childrenAges][&currency=USD]`, beta. Returns `data.amount` ("The best (lowest) cached public price amount for the stay"), `nightlyAmount`, `provider` (normalised id, e.g. `cheaptickets`), `rawObservedText`, `offers[] {amount, nightly, provider, rawProvider}`, `fetchedAt`, `expiresAt`. Sources are "public booking platforms (e.g., Booking.com, Expedia)"; the example lists CheapTickets, Expedia, Hotels.com, Orbitz, Travelocity, Booking.com, Priceline, Super.com and others. $0.02 a request, 10 requests a minute on both key types, 404 for cache misses and "no public price found". The docs do not say whether amounts include taxes and fees, nor the cache TTL. Not called today (outside the one approved live request). It is the only API-shaped source that names Booking.com/Expedia per hotel; whether its Booking.com offer equals Booking's checkout is a $0.20 probe (ten hotels) away. Its cost is not lower than Google's, and the discount-reseller entries would need the same all-in filter as today.

## 2. SerpAPI Google Hotels

Verified ([pricing](https://serpapi.com/pricing), [Google Hotels API](https://serpapi.com/google-hotels-api), [Property Details](https://serpapi.com/google-hotels-property-details)):

- Plans: Free 250 searches a month, 50 an hour; Starter $25 for 1,000 ($0.025); Developer $75 for 5,000 ($0.015); Production $150 for 15,000 ($0.010). Commercial use of the free plan is not addressed on the pricing, FAQ or legal pages.
- A search returns ~20 properties a page with `rate_per_night` and `total_rate` (each `lowest` and `before_taxes_fees`) for the lead offer, plus `prices[]` per source with `rate_per_night` only; no per-seller total in the search response.
- Property Details (`property_token`, one request per hotel) returns `prices[]` and `featured_prices[]` per seller with `source`, `official`, `rate_per_night` and `total_rate` each carrying `lowest` and `before_taxes_fees`, plus `free_cancellation`.

Inferred: every request counts as one search (the pricing counts "searches"; the FAQ and legal pages do not define it further). A 10-hotel comparison is 1 search + 10 details = 11 searches: free up to 22 comparisons a month, then $0.165–0.275 each. The data is Google's ladder, the same semantics as the current actor (Booking row all-in, Expedia/Hotels.com before tax with an estimate, discounters short), so reliability equals today's and cost is higher except on the free tier. Engineering: new provider, small parser, same name matching; one call per hotel.

## 3. Travelpayouts / Hotellook

Not verifiable from a primary source today: the support article "Hotels Data API" (203956163) sits behind Cloudflare's JavaScript challenge (403 from both WebFetch and curl with a browser UA). Secondary sources ([awskii/hotellook Go client](https://pkg.go.dev/github.com/awskii/hotellook), [travelpayouts_api Ruby gem](https://www.rubydoc.info/gems/travelpayouts_api/0.0.1/TravelPayouts/Api/Hotel), a [Travelpayouts support note on data-API access](https://support.travelpayouts.com/hc/en-us/articles/203956083-Requirements-for-Aviasales-data-API-access) via search snippet) describe `engine.hotellook.com/api/v2/lookup.json`, `yasen.hotellook.com/api/v2/cache.json` returning `priceFrom`/`priceAvg` per hotel from "the cache based on the user's search history", kept "from 2 to 7 days", free with a Travelpayouts token and marker. Live probe today: `lookup.json`, `cache.json` on both hosts returned nginx 404 without a token (a gated API answers 401/403; a 404 suggests the paths have moved or gone). Even working, the cached figure has no seller and no tax statement, so it fails the named-seller rule. The full `search/start.json` flow returns rooms with `AgencyName`, `Tax`, `Total` per the Go client, but it is an affiliate search engine with a marker, rate-limited, and undocumented in English today. Not viable.

## 4. Amadeus Self-Service Hotel Search v3

Verified from Amadeus's guides ([hotels guide](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/amadeus4dev/developer-guides/master/docs/resources/hotels.md), [pricing guide](https://developers.amadeus.com/self-service/apis-docs/guides/developer-guides/pricing)): the price object is `base`, `total`, `taxes[] {amount, included}`, `variations`; the example tax is `included: false`; prices are "per room"; inventory "comes from BEDBANK for aggregators and DIRECTCHAIN for GDS/Distribution"; the free monthly quota carries into production and extra calls are billed, but the per-call price list is a JavaScript page that did not render (secondary: "EUR 0.0008–0.025 per call", [supergood.ai](https://supergood.ai/api-report-card/amadeus), 20 Oct 2026). Reported, not verified against Amadeus directly: PhocusWire reported that Amadeus is decommissioning the Self-Service portal with keys disabled on 17 July 2026 and registration paused ([PhocusWire](https://www.phocuswire.com/amadeus-shut-down-self-service-apis-portal-developers), 403 to fetch; [Ignav](https://ignav.com/docs/amadeus-self-service-shutdown) and [AirLabs](https://airlabs.co/amadeus-self-service-api-shutdown) repeat it; the developer portal home is JavaScript-only). Either way a GDS/bedbank room rate with taxes possibly excluded is not a public seller's checkout total. Not viable.

## 5. Cheaper Apify actors

Prices read from the Store API (`GET https://api.apify.com/v2/store?search=…`, field `currentPricingInfo`) and the actors' store pages, 22 Oct 2026.

Booking.com:

- **`kestrel/booking-prices-scraper`** ([store page](https://apify.com/kestrel/booking-prices-scraper)): pay per event, `hotel` $0.004, `room` $0.002 (only with `includeRooms`); sold-out, unpriced and filtered rows free. Inputs: `locationQueries`, **`hotelNames`** ("uses Booking's autocomplete and prices the first match. Check `hotel_name` in the status row to confirm the match"), `hotelIds`, `startUrls`; `checkIn`, `nights` (1–30; no `checkOut`), `adults`, `rooms`, `currency` (default USD), `minStars`, `maxPrice`, `maxHotelsPerQuery` (default 50). Output per hotel: `name`, `url`, `price_total` ("the lowest stay total Booking shows for the party"), `price_nightly`, `taxes_included`, `taxes_fees_excluded`, `charges_note`, `free_cancellation`, `stars`, `review_score`, `sold_out`, `room_name`. The page states "in some countries the total includes taxes; elsewhere Booking lists them as excluded" and that the flags say which per row. Same developer as the actor in production; 23 users, 640 runs (young). Ten named hotels: $0.04.
- **`rl1987/booking-api-scraper`** ([store page](https://apify.com/rl1987/booking-api-scraper)): `search-result` $0.001, `hotel-details` $0.001, `room-details` $0.0001; no start fee. Calls Booking's mobile app API, not the website or the Demand API. Output `price.total` ("Booking's display price before the listed charges"), `price.extra_charges {included, excluded, details[]}`, **`price.total_with_charges` ("the all-in amount")**, `chargesCompleteness` ("incalculable" means more may apply). Caveats on the page: inventory differs from the site (5,844 vs 6,084 for Tokyo), mobile-only discounts, bursts fail and are retried six times, taxes-in-total depends on point of sale on the `hotel_ids` path. 355 users. Ten hotels by destination search: $0.01–0.05.
- `voyager/booking-scraper` (9.9k users): "from $2.00 / 1,000 scraped place results", `price` basis undocumented (example `"price": 1.118, "currency": "US$"`); would need a test run to establish whether it is the stay total. `cirkit/booking-search-scraper` $0.0005 an item with "price, taxes" fields, 14 users. `kestrel` and `rl1987` are the two that document the tax treatment.

Google Hotels:

- `kestrel/google-hotels-prices` (current): `hotel` $0.004, `offer` $0.002, status rows free; ~18 offer rows a hotel if uncapped ([store page](https://apify.com/kestrel/google-hotels-prices)). With `includeOffers: false` a hotel row costs $0.004 but names no seller, so it cannot be used alone.
- `martin.forejt/google-hotels-scraper`: $0.0001 an item plus $0.00005 a start; `prices[] {provider, price, link}` per hotel but no total, no tax field, currency unlabelled ([store page](https://apify.com/martin.forejt/google-hotels-scraper)). Fails all-in.
- `vittuhy/google-travel-hotel-prices`: $0.001 an item plus $0.005 a start; one item per provider per night, `price` nightly, taxes unstated, `entity` accepts a hotel name ([store page](https://apify.com/vittuhy/google-travel-hotel-prices)). Fails all-in.
- `s-r/free-google-hotels-scraper` `hotel_search` $0.003 / `hotel_detail` $0.005; `scrapercompany/google-hotels-property-scraper` $0.005 a property request; both are Google-ladder data at prices close to kestrel's; not examined further.

## Ranked recommendation

Ranked by reliability (a named seller's true checkout total for the exact hotel and dates, boutique coverage, freshness), cost as the tiebreaker.

1. **Booking.com's results page for each LiteAPI hotel by name, through `kestrel/booking-prices-scraper`.** It is the one source whose figure we have measured against the seller's own checkout (within $3), it is live, it looks up the exact hotel by name and returns the matched name for a `sameHotel` check, it flags per row whether taxes are in the total and by how much they are excluded, and it costs about $0.04 for ten hotels (~$4 a month at 100 comparisons, roughly a tenth of today). Booking.com lists nearly every European hotel, so boutique coverage should be at least Google's; measure it on the Courchevel nine before switching. The honest label becomes "public price on Booking.com" rather than "cheapest public price", which is what the all-in rule already reduces most rows to. Risks: a young actor (23 users) from the developer we already depend on; autocomplete picking a sister property (mitigated by the returned name check, and `hotelIds` for repeats); Booking showing taxes excluded in some markets (add `taxes_fees_excluded` to `price_total` and say so). Engineering: one new `PublicRatesProvider` (~60 lines) taking the hotel names from the LiteAPI rows, a trivial parser, cache per hotel and stay instead of per city, offline test with fake rows; half a day.
2. **Keep the Google ladder (`kestrel/google-hotels-prices`) as the fallback** for hotels Booking does not list, with today's rule: Booking.com row as-is, Expedia/Hotels.com plus Google's hotel-level tax estimate (within $11 today), discounters shown only as "lists X before taxes & fees". Reliable enough, $0.17–0.40 a run; SerpAPI is the same data for free under 22 comparisons a month and dearer above.
3. **`rl1987/booking-api-scraper`** as the backup if the kestrel Booking actor breaks: it is the cheapest with an explicit all-in field (`price.total_with_charges`), but it rides Booking's unofficial mobile API and its own page lists inventory gaps and burst failures.
4. **LiteAPI `GET /prices/public`**: worth one $0.20 probe on the Courchevel hotels to see whether its Booking.com `offers[]` amount matches Booking's checkout; if it does, it is a single-vendor path with no Apify dependency, at $0.02 a hotel and 10 requests a minute.

Not viable: LiteAPI's SSP (a 1.15 × floor, not a seller), Hotellook (dead endpoints, cached, no seller), Amadeus (Self-Service reportedly shut, GDS rates not public prices), DataForSEO/Xotelo/martin.forejt/vittuhy (nightly, no taxes), Makcorps ($350 a month), partner APIs (Booking Demand, Expedia Rapid, Google Hotel Center).

**Single cheapest option that meets the non-negotiables:** Booking.com by hotel name through `kestrel/booking-prices-scraper`, about $0.04 a ten-hotel comparison and $4 a month at 100, with the Google ladder kept only for hotels Booking does not list.

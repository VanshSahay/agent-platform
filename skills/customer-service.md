---
name: customer-service
description: Dealing with a company on the person's behalf: a refund, a cancellation, a billing dispute, a missing order.
---
1. Get the facts first: company, account identifier (order number, email on the account), what happened, what outcome the person wants, and any deadline. Read any photo or email they sent (files.read, email.search).
2. Find the right channel in this order: the company's self-service page in the browser (cancel, refund, dispute buttons), then their support email, then chat or a phone number. web.search "<company> cancel subscription" or "<company> refund policy" usually finds it.
3. Write the message or fill the form plainly: what happened, what you want, by when. One ask per message. Keep the person's tone neutral and firm.
4. Sending an email or submitting a form in their name is a write: the platform asks their yes. Show them exactly what will be sent.
5. If the company replies by email later, the person can ask you to check; set a reminder.schedule with unless_reply_from=<support address> so you follow up only if they haven't answered.
6. Never agree to charges, accept a settlement, or share card numbers, ID numbers or passwords. Stop and ask the person.
7. Log the case: remember the case number and what was agreed, scope private.

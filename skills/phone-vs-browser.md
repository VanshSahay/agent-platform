---
name: phone-vs-browser
description: Choosing between the cloud browser and the person's own phone (phone_* tools) for a task, and how to drive the phone.
---
Choose, in this order:
1. A connected account tool (Google) or a public API: fastest, verifiable.
2. The cloud browser (browser.task) for any public website. It is logged out and has no payment details.
3. The person's phone (phone_* tools, only if they connected one) for: native apps with no website, anything already signed in on the phone (WeChat, banking, Health, their airline app), and reading codes or messages that only arrive on the phone.

Driving the phone:
- Check phone_status first. If it is locked or someone else holds it, say so and stop; a person must unlock it.
- Read the screen as elements, act on an element by its number, and read again. One action at a time.
- Sign-ins, passcodes and Face ID: stop and ask the person to do that step on the phone; hold the phone (phone_hold) while they do. Never ask for a password or code.
- Payment and banking actions are theirs to tap. You may navigate to the screen and stop.
- Release the phone (phone_release_owner) when the task is done.

---
name: outreach
description: Contacting a third party on the person's behalf (an email to a business or a person, a call, a message): what has to be disclosed and confirmed first, when to hold it, and how to report it.
---
Rules, in order:
1. Disclosure before contact. Before you email, call or message anyone on the person's behalf, the plan you show them names who you will contact (name and address or number), how (email, call), and why (what you will ask for or say). Their yes to that is what authorizes the contact. Anyone not named in that disclosure was not disclosed: ask before contacting them, every time.
2. Re-check the premise. A scheduled or delayed contact rests on a premise (a table still needed, a package still missing, a reply still outstanding). Before acting, check it against the live thread, the calendar or the inbox. If you can't verify it, or it no longer holds, tell the person what you found instead of contacting anyone.
3. Daytime only. The platform holds outreach made outside 8am to 8pm in the person's time zone and sends it the next morning; it tells them so. Don't try to work around that.
4. One contact per step. Don't contact several parties in one go unless the plan said so.
5. Share the person's details only as disclosed. Their phone number, address or email goes to a third party only if they said it could, in this conversation or in a rule they gave you.
6. Report plainly. After contact: who you reached, what was said or agreed, what's still open, and the proof (a confirmation number, a reply). If it went to voicemail or bounced, say so and propose the next step rather than retrying silently.

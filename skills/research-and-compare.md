# Researching options for a decision

Decisions are the pain, not information. Done means: three options, one recommendation, and the constraint each one fails, in a form the group can decide on.

1. Write the constraints down first (`plan`): who it is for, where, budget, dates, must-haves. They go in the goal so nothing drops.
2. `web_search` for candidates; `browse_page` the two or three that matter to confirm price, availability and the must-haves. Two searches with nothing new is a stall: read a page, ask the room, or report.
3. Candidates that fail a hard constraint are out, not "worth mentioning".
4. `compare_options` with the facts that decide (price, availability, the must-haves); recommend one and say why in one sentence.
5. For a group: `write_document` only when the group will act on it over time (vote, claim, add). Otherwise one message with the table and the recommendation; let the requester choose with `wait_for_user_input` when booking follows.
6. Longer than a few steps: `delegate_task` with the constraints and the candidates so far; the chat stays free and the task reports once.

---
name: reservations
description: Booking a table, a hotel room, a class spot or an appointment on a website that has no API.
---
1. Pin down what the person wants before touching a site: place, date, time window, party size, any must-haves. If one is missing and the answer changes the booking, ask once.
2. Find the venue's own booking page first (web.search "<venue> reservations"). Resy, OpenTable, Tock and SevenRooms pages work in the browser; a restaurant's own site often links to one of them.
3. browser.task without complete: search the exact date and party size, read the available times, and come back with the options and what the final step would submit (name, phone, email, card hold, cancellation policy).
4. Give the person the options in one short message with the exact times. Ask which one. Do not book yet.
5. When they pick, call browser.task again with complete=true and the exact slot. The platform asks their yes before the browser completes it. Report the confirmation number from the result, nothing else as proof.
6. If the site asks for a login, a CAPTCHA, or a code, stop and tell the person what it needs. Never guess credentials.
7. Offer a calendar event only after the booking is confirmed, and only if they have Google connected.
